Offline AI Notepad

A beautiful notepad with AI built in — and nothing you write ever leaves your iPhone or iPad. No account. No subscription. No internet needed.

Formats as you type

Real checkboxes you tick with a tap, bullets and numbered lists that continue themselves, nested lists, headings, quotes, bold, italic and more. Type [] and a checklist appears.

AI that works offline

Summarize, Key points, Polish, Shorter, Longer, Formal, Friendly, Simplify, Bullets, Continue, suggest a Title, and Translate into seven languages — all on the device.

Ask your notes

Ask a question across everything you've written. The app finds the right notes, the AI answers from them, and the exact sentence from your note is shown underneath so you can check it.

Write with AI

Describe what you need — a packing list, a meeting agenda, study notes, an email — and a draft appears. It's only saved if you keep it.

Folders, tags and photos

File notes in folders, tag them with #words to filter in a tap, and add photos from your library. Search highlights what it found inside the note.

Five versions, like git

Every note keeps its last five versions. See exactly what changed, word by word, and go back with one tap — including after any AI edit. Undo and redo cover every step.

Honest about AI

A compact language model runs on your device's own chip. It's fast and private, and like any AI it can be wrong — so nothing changes until you choose, rewrites show their changes first, figures the AI added are flagged, and every change can be undone.

Private by design

No account, no sync, no analytics, no tracking and no network connections at all. Your notes, photos and history are stored only on your device.

Two finishes

Emerald — brushed metallic emerald with gold highlights and silver-white text. Dark — brushed graphite with silver highlights and light grey text.

For iPhone and iPad

On iPad your notes and the open note sit side by side. Works in flight mode, on the underground, anywhere.
